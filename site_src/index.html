Earthquakes, cyclones, floods, droughts and disaster mapping, as the issuing authorities report them.

USGS earthquakes with their PAGER impact alert, GDACS multi-hazard alerts, NOAA National Hurricane Center storms and Copernicus EMS rapid-mapping activations, collected every hour.

Alerts above the bottom rung

PAGER yellow or worse, and GDACS orange or red. Both are model estimates of impact, issued within minutes and revised as reports arrive. A GDACS alert is the highest the event has reached.
